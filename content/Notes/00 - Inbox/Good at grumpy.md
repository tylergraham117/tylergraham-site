# Good at grumpy

This might come as a surprise, but I am really good at being in a bad mood. An absolute expert. I’ve even figured out how to get storm clouds to follow me around like a pet. It’s pretty serious stuff.

Jrnie does not stand for my bad moods. If were together, and I'm stuck in something, 
She turns to me and says in a tone as pure as rain, "why are you so grumpy?"

Why *am* I so grumpy? Something about that word feels like it belongs on one a 90 year old man in a cartoon, not on me. It's immediately disarming.
