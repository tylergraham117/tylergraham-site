# Karl Shakur's Content Strategy


## Content Types
> [Meet the creator who got paid 70k to quit his job forever ft. Karl Shakur - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YuajOb9rz_Y))

### Play
This is stuff you make for fun. It can be anything you want.

### Process
BTS, tricks you use in your process, tutorials, that sort of thing. It's a peak behind the curtain.

### Pitch
This is a showcase for prospective clients. Maybe it's a spec or some sort of product placement. The idea is to do something that shows clients what you can do for them.

### Portfolio
This is a look into what's going on in your business life. 

### Personal
These are projects that give insight into your personal life. It shows the audience who you are beyond the work.
