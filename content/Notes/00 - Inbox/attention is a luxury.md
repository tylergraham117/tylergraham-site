# attention is a luxury

What makes something luxurious? Is it actually the scarcity or the price? 

I think maybe another huge component is how the luxury good/experience/service makes us feel. 
A large part of a luxury is the status is conveys. 


So is attention a luxury? It is definitely limited. When we spend out attention on things that are not necessary for existing, we are making a choice. 

If you make a thing that requires people's attention to consume, you're saying that consuming that thing is more valuable, at least to a certain group of people, that all the other options they have available at that moment. That's a lot of pressure.
