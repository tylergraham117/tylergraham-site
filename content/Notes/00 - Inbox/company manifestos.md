# company manifestos

Lots of tech companies create manifestos as a guide for what they believe and how they operate. While it feels a little self-important and overly serious, I like the idea of have some [[clearly defined values]] and beliefs, and declaring those to the world. It helps people figure out who you are for and who you are not.


## Some Examples:

- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/about)
	- One page. Simple. Clear.
- [Manifesto — Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so/manifesto)
	- A little overboard. Definitely made to be an experience. Even so, it fits the brand.
