# gordon castle mug

I recently got to stay in a castle. It's easy to get swept up in the grandeur of something like that. I mean it's a castle. 

But my favorite part about the castle wasn't the large estate or the beautifully kept grounds or the looming tower.
It was the small details. The care and attention.

For example, every morning we would wake up and have breakfast. Coffee was served in these quaint little mugs. And throughout the day, there would be fresh coffee and tea right next to a pile of mugs, ready for anyone needing a pick me up.

I need a lot of pick me ups, so there was always a mug fused to my hand.

That magical trip has passed, and I'm settled back into my normal, non-royal lifestyle. But I got to take one of those mugs home. 

Memories are a funny thing. We store them in objects, in smells, in other people even. My home is filled with storied objects that house memories.

Every time I pick up this mug, my hands instantly connect it's dainty handle and clean roundness to that experience and all of the wonderful people I shared it with.
