# the elephant rider

Behavior change can be hard. Psychologist Jonathan Haidt came up with a metaphor to explain why.

Think of your logical, thinking brain as a person on top of an elephant. The elephant represents your emotional and subconscious drivers.

The driver can point the elephant in the direction all they want, but if the elephant doesn't want to go there, you can't force it.
