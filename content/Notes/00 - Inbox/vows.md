# vows






I fell in love with you when I was 21 years old. I had no beard and looked like I had just stepped off the school bus. 

It's hard to remember what was going through the mind of that version of myself. 

What I do remember is that before I knew you, I had all these ideas about how I thought the world worked. 

I liked to read about people who did incredible things. I thought if I heard enough stories or made enough plans, eventually I'd turn into that type of person. 

Meeting you completely wrecked my entire world view. 

Not because of something you said or did, but because of who you are.

You took my very best ideas about who a person could be right out of my imagination and put them into real life. And then you added a bunch of extra stuff I couldn't even dream up.

You showed me that we don't need to study incredible people. We can just be them.

The way you show up for people is contagious. You make a conscious effort to spread joy and wonder and encouragement and gratitude.

You set the tone of every room you walk into, and it seems like every interaction you have leaves people feeling acknowledge and cared for because you meet everyone with an open heart and an open mind. 




I know that you didn't become the incredible person you are by yourself, and it feels like such an honor to know the people who’ve worked so hard to shape you into person I love so much.

I've never known anyone to have as deep of a connection with their family as you do. 
Acknowledging what I love about you without acknowledging how your family has shaped you would be missing the entire foundation of who you are.

From your dad I see the strongest sense of self of anyone I’ve ever known.
From your mom I see the ability to make the people around you feel like they are the most important person in the world.
From your grandma I see the ability to find joy in every part of life and to never let the hard things get to your head, otherwise you might get a headache.
And from your grandpa, I see a deep self reflection and spiritual foundation that is rare and elemental.





Over the years, you've asked me a few times how I would describe you to a stranger.

I always struggled a little bit answering that, and not because I don't know enough about you.

It's just that instead of a collection of facts, I'm thinking about how impossible it is to paraphrase someone I feel like I know so fully. 


In those moments I'm thinking about how you wake up each day with this intense energy and excitement for life, and how you dance in the kitchen to no music while I make coffee and try to keep up, and how that feels like such a fitting metaphor for our life together.

I'm thinking about the way you make people feel so loved and cared for by remembering every little detail about the stories they tell you.

I'm thinking about finding myself in the middle of the ocean swimming with sharks because you decided to get interested in sharks, and when you get curious about something you become so laser focused and driven that nothing in the world could stop you.

I'm thinking about how you put together the most thoughtful gifts I've ever seen and then still worry if it's good enough because you love the people in your life so much that nothing could ever be good enough for them. 

I'm thinking about running around a pop up carnival with you for hours trying to find $7, and how you fearlessly and joyfully pursue everything in your life the same as you did those ferris wheel tickets.

I'm thinking about how you make sure anyone that steps into our home has 10 different beverages and how that small, conscious act of generosity creates a safe and loving environment.

I'm think about how you burst into my office in the middle of each day because the next room over was much too far and you needed some time to reconnect, and how special it is that we can be together and separate at the same time. 

I'm thinking about how you'll befriend a complete stranger who you meet in passing in a different country and build such a strong relationship in that short time that you continue talking to them long after we've left.

I'm thinking about how when you tell me a story, you lean into your childhood acting experience to put on a performance that makes me feel like I was there.

I'm thinking about how you treat me like I'm the most extraordinary person in the world, which is simultaneously wholly undeserved and fills me with a deep sense of confidence and courage in who I am and who I'm becoming. 

And when I'm sitting there thinking about how to try and summarize the whole of your entire being, I'm left with an overwhelming sense of gratitude that I get to know and understand and being present with someone so singular.





You've taught me what it means to take risk. Not a physical danger type of risk, but a risk of open heartedness.
It's walking out onto a branch to reach for something or someone, knowing full well it could snap beneath you. It's an earnest trust in possibility, and a full commitment to who you dream of becoming.


You've taught me that the words we use matter and every conversation is an opportunity to show people we love them. Which, coming from a friend group who's love language was bullying, was a much needed lesson.


You've shown me that living in fear is boring and unoriginal and that it's much more interesting to lean into situations that make you feel uncomfortable. 


You've taught me that actions are louder than intentions, and you show me every day that love is active.


You bring out the version of me that I'm most proud of.







I vow to not only to enthusiastically encourage your dreams, but to actively help make them come true.

I vow to never stop exploring and adventuring with you. 

I vow to be aware of how my thoughts and words shape our lives.

I vow to lead a life driven more by curiosity than fear.

I vow to not get overly attached to who you are in any given season and to instead help you become the person you're becoming and the person you want to be.

I vow that if you have something that fills you with joy and decide to bring it to me, I will pick it up and share it with you and do my absolute best not to drop it. 

I vow to love you irrationally and express it frequently.

I vow to celebrate with you often.

I vow to come to our relationship as energetic as I can possibly be.

I vow to create an environment of trust and security.

I vow to be playful.

I vow to chase whimsy with you. 








Finally, I vow to not get used to this. 

What I mean by that is I believe that love is comes from time and understanding and sacrifice and commitment. 

I promise to be committed to the never ending process of intentional care that is necessary to keep the shininess of love from fading. I promise to never let our relationship become automated, because as soon as it does it loses its soul, and that will not happen here.

I promise to renew this vows each day through my actions, and I expect to be held to them.
