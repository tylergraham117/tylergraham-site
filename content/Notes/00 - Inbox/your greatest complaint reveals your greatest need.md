# your greatest complaint reveals your greatest need

The things we spend the most time complaining about often reveal our deepest needs, as long as we're willing to dig a little.

According to Mike Foster, the greatest complaint towards your partner usually reveals your greatest need. It seems like people have a tendency to transfer problems rather than teaming up to solve them.
