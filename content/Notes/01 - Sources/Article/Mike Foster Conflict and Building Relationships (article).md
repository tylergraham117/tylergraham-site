# Mike Foster Conflict and Building Relationships (article)

We don't hear each other, we imagine what the other person said.

The greatest complaint towards your partner usually reveals your greatest need.

Team up in problems rather than transferring them.

Stop asking "what's wrong?" It sets the energy to negative by default.

Instead, ask "what's needed?" It's inherently more positive.

Criticism pushes you away from people.

Good feedback begins with empathy, moves to curiosity, and ends with collaboration. 

**Fight Styles**
Godzilla vs Turtle (loud and confrontation vs quiet reserved and processing)
Teddy bear fight style (soft strong warm comforting)


What brought you Joy or happiness this week?

What was a challenge for us in the relationship this week?

Did we have any communication missteps this week? Clarify and move to a healthier place. 
Gives the partner benefit of the doubt.

What's one specific thing I can do for you this week?
