# Primal Question with Mike Foster


## Why Most Marriage Advice Doesn't Work

1. Cliches instead of truth.
2. Level of investment doesn't match level of the problems.
3. Working on symptoms, not problems.

## Core Driver

Everyone has a set of core needs. When these needs aren't met, we enter into what Mike calls a "Scramble." Most of us are in relationships with each other's Scramble, not each other.

### What are The Seven Primal Questions?

1. Am I safe? — The need for physical and emotional safety
2. Am I secure? - The need for enough financial and relational resources
3. Am I loved? - The need to be seen, heard, and known
4. Am I wanted? - The need to be pursued and connected
5. Am I successful? - The need to feel like a winner and achieve success
6. Am I good enough? - The need to feel worthy and valued
7. Do I have a purpose? - The need to have an impact and meaning in life

> The core idea is that if your answer to your primal question is "no," you cannot be in a healthy relationship.
