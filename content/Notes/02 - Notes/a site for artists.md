# a site for artists

I would love to make a website for artists and creators and makers. It would be community focused, providing connection to other creatives as well as a place to learn and grow. 
It would be funded by selling software products, like editing plugins and packs, but it would also have lots of free stuff. Similar to the Crow Hill model.

What is it that we're making? They're tools for artists. "We make things for you to make things with."
